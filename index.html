Climb and descend
Hold THRUST to climb. Release it to descend.
Recover salvage
Get close to an item and tap TETHER. Stay nearby while it reels in.
Bank your haul
Align with the station and hold DEPOSIT. Items bank one at a time; carried cargo does not count toward completed objectives.
Brake early
Fast, heavy loads carry momentum. Release thrust to slow a climb; hold thrust to slow a descent. Avoid both boundaries and watch suit integrity.
Earn money for upgrades
Play Contracts to build your wallet: every deposit pays the salvage value, and completing the job adds its bonus once per run. Replay contracts to earn more. Ordinary campaign deposits and Endless scores do not add money to your wallet; completing each world’s campaign awards a one-time $2,000 bonus.
Use your upgrades
Spend wallet money in Upgrades. Purchased gear applies to Campaign, Contracts, and Endless when you start your next run.
Keep what you bank
Contract earnings already deposited stay in your wallet if you fail or leave. Unbanked cargo is lost.
Choose your world
Use the arrows on the world page, then choose its Campaign, Contracts, or Endless mode. Each world keeps its own Endless best.

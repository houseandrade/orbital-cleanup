# Mars art review — v1

First artwork pass requested by Brian on September 13, 2026. Artwork approved by Brian. Background, sample canister and survey drone integrated in the first Mars playtest; remaining sprites reserved for later missions. Created with the built-in image generation tool. Original PNGs and alpha channels preserved. Review index.html for enlarged sprites, 32/48px samples, and proposed portrait terrain placement.

Style reference: src/art/lunar/instrument-package.png for salvage; src/art/lunar/moon-background.png for background composition. Cream, charcoal, metallic gray and teal hardware against red Martian terrain. Item sizes and gameplay roles remain proposals.

## mars-background

Asset: mars-background.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a landscape 1536x1024 opaque pixel-art Mars environment background for Orbital Cleanup. Input Moon image is STYLE AND COMPOSITION reference only. Replace gray lunar terrain with a broad gently curved rust-red Martian horizon rising to about 55% canvas height at center, lower at edges; terrain fills bottom edge. Dark brick red and burnt sienna with restrained copper highlights, broad shallow craters, eroded ridges and a few large dark rock clusters, crisp stepped pixel clusters matching the reference. Suggest an abandoned expedition site through two tiny low cream foundations and an unfinished dark support structure far below the horizon, subordinate to terrain. Upper half near-black navy empty space with just a few faint stars. No Earth or other large planet. Very subtle thin dusty rim at horizon only, no bright haze. Keep center sky dark and empty for gameplay readability. No mountains projecting into sky, no astronaut, floating salvage, station, HUD, text or watermark. Design terrain to remain legible when reduced to a 360px-wide strip at bottom of a portrait game canvas.
